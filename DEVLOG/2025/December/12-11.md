## Accomplishments

###### *What did I actually complete?*

* RoomScanner script will scan the room into planes using the ARPlane manager attached to an XR Origin. Then it'll periodically send those planes via the server so they can be rendered on the desktop.
  * Scanner will also assign each plane a "type" according to its features: floor, ceiling, wall, or item.
* Room viewer will render the room and assign each mesh a mat according to its type.

## Challenges & Blockers

###### *What problems did I run into? Even small frustrations are worth noting.*

* The RoomScanner is sending every mesh in the room every second, this makes the packets extremely large and the program should be recoded such that only planes that have just been updated or added are sent to the viewer.
* When the floor or ceiling is modified then the scanner doesn't recognize it sometimes and ends up just changing the mesh type to "object"
  * Fixed this by manually tracking the floor and ceiling plane in the RoomScanner and whenever they're modified make sure to give them the "floor" or "ceiling" type.
* Scanner can't tell the difference between a wall and a vertical item such as a monitor or divider.
